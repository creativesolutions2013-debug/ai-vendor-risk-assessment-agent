# Simulated AI-Assisted Assessment

> **Simulation notice:** This output was generated by a mock provider for
> testing and portfolio demonstration. No external AI service was used.

## Executive Summary

NorthStar Analytics presents a preliminary **High inherent risk** because
the service is business-critical and processes personal information and
confidential business data.

The rules-based assessment identified four control gaps. High-risk findings
require validation and approval by an authorized human reviewer.

## Priority Findings

### 1. Independent Penetration Testing

The vendor has not completed an independent penetration test. The stated
120-day implementation commitment is planned remediation and does not
demonstrate that the control currently operates.

**Follow-up evidence request:**

- Independent penetration-test executive summary
- Scope and testing dates
- Severity of identified findings
- Remediation status for high-risk findings

**Human decision required:** Determine whether interim safeguards and the
proposed remediation period sufficiently reduce the engagement risk.

### 2. Endpoint Detection and Response

EDR is not currently deployed. The 30-day commitment must not be treated as
an implemented control.

**Follow-up evidence request:**

- EDR deployment report
- Endpoint coverage percentage
- Detection and response configuration
- Evidence of operational monitoring

**Human decision required:** Determine whether production use should be
restricted until EDR implementation is validated.

### 3. Security Log Retention

The vendor retains security logs for 30 days rather than the required
180-day period.

**Follow-up evidence request:**

- Logging and monitoring policy
- Current platform-retention configuration
- Implementation evidence for 180-day retention

### 4. Subprocessor Risk Management

The vendor reported only partial implementation of its subprocessor-risk
process.

**Follow-up evidence request:**

- Current subprocessor inventory
- Security-review procedure
- Evidence of completed subprocessor assessments
- Customer notification process for material changes

## Preliminary Residual Risk

The preliminary residual-risk rating remains **High** until applicable
high-risk findings are remediated, supporting evidence is validated, and
the authorized risk owner approves the final treatment.

## Human Review Requirements

A qualified reviewer must:

- Validate all referenced evidence
- Confirm control applicability
- Review the severity of each finding
- Approve remediation periods
- Determine whether compensating controls are sufficient
- Make the final vendor-approval and risk-acceptance decisions

## Assumptions and Limitations

- The vendor and assessment data are fictional.
- No evidence was independently authenticated.
- Planned remediation was not treated as implemented.
- This simulated output is not a final risk decision.
