# Vendor Risk Assessment Instructions

## Objective

Evaluate the supplied fictional vendor profile against every applicable
requirement in the third-party security control library.

Base the assessment only on the supplied information. Do not assume that
missing information represents an implemented control.

## Assessment Tasks

1. Summarize the vendor’s service, data access, integration, and business
   criticality.
2. Assign an inherent-risk rating of Low, Moderate, High, or Critical.
3. Explain the factors supporting the inherent-risk rating.
4. Determine which controls in the control library apply.
5. Evaluate the vendor against each applicable control.
6. Identify control gaps, missing evidence, contradictions, and planned
   remediation.
7. Generate follow-up questions for unresolved issues.
8. Draft risk statements and recommendations for material findings.
9. Assign a preliminary residual-risk rating.
10. Identify all decisions requiring human review.

## Control Evaluation Format

For every control, provide:

- Control ID
- Control domain
- Applicability
- Assessment status
- Vendor response
- Supporting evidence
- Missing evidence
- Risk severity
- Recommended action
- Human review required: Yes or No

## Finding Format

### Finding Title

**Control ID:**  
**Severity:**  
**Assessment status:**

#### Condition

Describe the current control weakness or missing evidence.

#### Risk statement

Because [control weakness], there is a risk that [threat or adverse event]
could occur, resulting in [business, operational, security, regulatory, or
reputational impact].

#### Recommendation

The vendor should [specific remediation action] by [target date or approved
milestone]. The organization should verify completion using [required
evidence].

#### Interim treatment

Identify any reasonable interim safeguard, monitoring requirement,
contractual requirement, or usage restriction.

#### Human review

Identify the approval or decision required from an authorized person.

## Important Constraints

- A planned control is not an implemented control.
- A policy statement alone does not prove operating effectiveness.
- Do not invent evidence or questionnaire responses.
- Identify insufficient information instead of guessing.
- Do not automatically lower a risk rating because the vendor has promised
  future remediation.
- Do not make the final vendor-approval or risk-acceptance decision.
